## 1. Diagnostic review (current materials)

### Verdict

PHAROS has a **strong conceptual mark** (lighthouse as orientation under uncertainty) and a **split visual system**: the public site reads cool institutional blue + teal light; the workbench reads warm paper + violet. Both can be PHAROS if the **system** is named; right now they behave like cousins, not one house.

### Strongest signal

The **lighthouse thesis** from the manifesto is the real identity asset — not “premium AI,” not “ethics theater”:

> A lighthouse doesn’t calm the sea… It makes the danger perceptible.

That is distinct in AI-governance: **perception + contestability**, not safety theater or startup salvation.

### Strategic mismatch

| Surface | What it signals today | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **pharos-ai.ca** | Procurement / audit blue; Sora + Manrope + Plex Mono; gradient CTAs; teal lantern | Serious and reviewable — but drifts toward **generic “govtech blue”** if proof isn’t louder than chrome |
| **Workbench** | Paper field, purple accent, serif title, short operator copy | Feels like a **scholar’s desk** — fits hybrid intent, but claims “standard PHAROS palette” while diverging from the public site |
| **Manifesto voice** | Literary, structural, anti-brand-as-admiration | Not yet fully carried into UI chrome (marketing site still uses uppercase mono pills) |

### Operational risk

- **Two palettes without a rule** → slides, papers, tools, and the site look like different companies.
- **Lighthouse icon alone** is category-adjacent (nav / maritime / civic apps); without **typo + restraint + proof architecture**, the mark becomes decoration.
- **Purple on paper** can read “creative agency” or “AI product purple” if pressure isn’t locked to *needs / action*, not decoration.

### Redesign priority

1. One **master system** with **register switches** (public brand vs operator product), not two brands.  
2. Decide **primary chromatic authority**: cool blue-teal *or* paper-violet *or* a dual system with explicit roles.  
3. Carry manifesto **restraint** into UI: fewer tracked eyebrows, fewer gradient pills, more document-compatible type.

### Specific next moves

1. Confirm the brief below (or correct it).  
2. Pick **one** of the three identity directions (or hybrid with a written rule).  
3. Emit mini style guide + align workbench tokens to that rule.  
4. Only then: logo refinement / mark monochrome tests.

## 2. Brand brief

### Audience

| Layer | Who | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buys / decides** | Procurement, audit, CGTO/CISO-adjacent, executive oversight under Law 25 / EU AI Act pressure | Needs reconstructible evidence, named decision rights |
| **Influences** | Legal, risk, scholarly peers, InfraFabric / method collaborators | Reads for judgment quality, not hype |
| **Uses daily (workbench)** | Martin (operator) — capture / prioritize / act under desk light | Needs speed, links, quiet urgency |
| **Public readers** | People who meet PHAROS via papers, site, manifesto | Need orientation, not a product demo |

### Offer (operational)

PHAROS is a **research-and-practice AI accountability house**: constraint-first method, stated thresholds, named decision rights, reconstructible review packets — plus a scholarly corpus that refuses self-certifying fluency.

**Workbench** is an **internal operator board** for method work + EMERAULD pointers — product surface that must still *smell like* PHAROS.

### Differentiation

Not “AI ethics content.” Not “another multi-agent platform brand.”

**Differentiation:** *Inspection-ready orientation under uncertainty* — make danger and decision structure **perceptible**, keep responsibility **traceable**, refuse fluency-as-proof.

Mechanism: lighthouse metaphor is structural (light maintained by someone; navigator retains agency; failure is recorded), not decorative.

### Buying / engagement context

High-trust, late-cycle, scrutiny-heavy. Documents, slides, and review packets matter as much as the homepage. Montréal / bilingual capability is part of institutional fit (EN-CA / FR-CA on site).

### Trust requirement

Serious, literate, defensible, **stable under re-reading**. Can be warm in human prose; UI must stay **calm and inspectable**. Trust ≠ softness; trust ≠ luxury.

### Brand adjectives (locked candidates)

**Quiet · Exact · Urgent** (operator / workbench personality, confirmed)

Extended house set for public brand (inferred, confirm):

**Lucid · Severe · Contestable · Steady**

### Avoid list

- Startup neon, AI mesh gradients, “future of work” cliché  
- Ethics-theater soft-focus (stock diverse handshake, vague “responsible AI”)  
- Notion second-brain sprawl; toy to-do gamification  
- Law-firm gold + navy without a distinct thesis  
- Luxury codes that sell exclusivity over inspectability  
- Noisy symbolism (overdrawn lighthouse, ray explosions, 3D glass towers)  
- Tech jargon on human surfaces (D1, cookie names, stack pride)

### How it should feel

**Emotional effect:** the room gets quieter; the stakes get clearer.  
**Formality:** high for packets and public site; medium-low density for operator tools; never casual-cute.

### Visual thesis (working)

**Controlled paper or cool field + one charged accent + typography that survives PDF export.**  
One symbolic logic (lighthouse as **orientation**, not product mascot).  
Accent used for **primary action and charged zones only** (needs, go, submit) — not decoration.

## 3. Identity directions (choose one)

### Direction A — *Cool Beacon* (close to live pharos-ai.ca)

- **Premise:** Institutional review room. Blue field of authority; teal is the *light* (the one charged moment).  
- **Visual cues:** Ink `#091225` / `#10203f`; field `#f3f6fc`; action blue `#1b4dff`; light teal `#0eb9a5`. Display **Sora**, body **Manrope**, mono **IBM Plex Mono** for codes and thresholds. Lighthouse mark in white on blue square.  
- **Why it fits:** Matches procurement / audit buyers; already deployed on pharos-ai.ca; monochrome mark possible.  
- **Risks:** Category default (govtech blue). Uppercase mono pills tip into SaaS. Weak continuity with warm paper tools unless tools inherit cool neutrals.

### Direction B — *Paper Pressure* (close to workbench)

- **Premise:** Scholar’s desk under deadline. Warm paper is the body; **violet is the beam** (method / book purple). Serif wordmark for PHAROS; sans for UI.  
- **Visual cues:** Field `#faf9f7`; ink `#1c1b1a`; accent `#6d28d9`; soft `#57534e`. Title **Iowan / Palatino / Georgia**; UI **Inter** (or Manrope for cross-surface). Almost no gradient.  
- **Why it fits:** Hybrid product + artifact; manifesto literary grain; Diamond Eyes quiet/exact/urgent; document-native PDFs and manuscripts.  
- **Risks:** Can read “creative studio” or “AI product purple” if accent floods. Less “procurement blue” at first glance — must compensate with proof architecture and severe type.

### Direction C — *Dual Register System* (recommended)

- **Premise:** One house, two rooms. **Public brand = Cool Beacon.** **Operator tools = Paper Pressure.** Shared: lighthouse logic, severe type scale, same ink hierarchy rules, same “accent only on action.”  
- **Visual cues:**  
  - Public: blue field + teal light + Sora/Manrope/Plex  
  - Operator: paper + violet beam + Inter + serif wordmark  
  - Shared: PHAROS wordmark rules, lighthouse geometry, mono for codes/thresholds only, radius ≤12px, no gradient text  
- **Why it fits:** Explains the existing split without pretending it’s one palette. Matches hybrid workbench PRODUCT.md and public site reality.  
- **Risks:** Needs a one-page rule so agents/designers don’t invent a third palette. Cross-surface decks must pick a room deliberately.

## 4. Mini style guide (interim — pending direction lock)

### Primary mark

- **Concept:** Simplified lighthouse (tower + lantern + optional single beam).  
- **Meaning:** Orientation under uncertainty; light is maintained; danger stays perceptible.  
- **Not:** Calm sea, safe harbor, product mascot, animated rays as default.

### Alternates

- Full color on field  
- Monochrome ink on paper  
- Reverse white on ink / blue  
- Wordmark-only for dense docs  

### Palette roles (until lock)

| Role | Cool Beacon | Paper Pressure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field | `#f3f6fc` | `#faf9f7` |
| Ink | `#091225` / `#10203f` | `#1c1b1a` |
| Soft | `#304c78` | `#57534e` |
| Accent / action | `#1b4dff` | `#6d28d9` |
| Light / success pulse | `#0eb9a5` | `#0f7a52` |
| Danger | keep crimson family | `#9f1239` |

### Typography

- **Public:** Sora (display) · Manrope (body) · IBM Plex Mono (thresholds, codes, CTAs sparingly)  
- **Operator:** Inter (UI) · serif for **Pharos** title only · mono for IDs/keys only  
- Scale product 1.125–1.2; no display letter-spacing tighter than −0.04em  
- Prefer sentence case over tracked uppercase eyebrows

### Spacing & components

- Radius 8–12px (cards/inputs); pills only for true chips/tags  
- Shadow: hairline or none — no ghost-card (border + 16px+ blur)  
- No side-stripe accent borders; no gradient text  
- One charged zone per screen (needs / primary CTA)

### Voice

- Plain nouns. Link as unit of work.  
- Manifesto can be literary; UI must not perform literature.  
- Prefer “What needs you” over “Your AI governance command center.”

### Dos / Don’ts

| Do | Don’t |
| --- | --- |
| Put proof and decision rights early | Lead with mesh/AI wallpaper |
| Use accent for action | Tint every card purple/blue |
| Export clean to PDF/print | Rely on glass blur for hierarchy |
| Keep lighthouse geometry simple | Overdraw rays and 3D towers |
| Name who owns the decision | Hide ownership in passive voice |

## 7. Decision — LOCKED

**Chosen: C — Dual Register System** (Martin, 2026-08-08).

### House rule (never invent a third palette)

| Room | Surface | Field | Action accent | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cool Beacon** | pharos-ai.ca, public decks for buyers, procurement packets when “institutional blue” helps | `#f3f6fc` | Blue `#1b4dff` · light pulse teal `#0eb9a5` | Sora · Manrope · IBM Plex Mono |
| **Paper Pressure** | Workbench, EMERAULD-adjacent ops UI, method desk tools | `#faf9f7` | Violet beam `#6d28d9` | Inter (UI) · serif wordmark only · mono for codes |

### Shared across rooms

1. Lighthouse = **orientation under uncertainty**, not mascot cosplay.  
2. **Accent only on action** (primary CTA, needs zone, Go links) — never every card.  
3. No gradient text; no side-stripe decoration; radius ≤12px on cards.  
4. Mono reserved for thresholds, codes, short status — not body.  
5. Proof / named decision rights before chrome.  
6. Decks and PDFs **pick a room deliberately** and stay in it for the whole artifact.
