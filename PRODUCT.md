# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Martin — one operator. Context is mid-flow PHAROS work: email invoices, meetings, paper stages, vault notes. Opens the board on workers.dev (or a local wrangler dev) under ordinary desk light, often between other tools. Not a multi-tenant team product; density and personal shortcuts beat onboarding theater.

## Product Purpose

A durable personal work board for PHAROS method work and EMERAULD (Git) pointers. One surface to **capture** (tasks with action links from mail/paste), **prioritize** (lists, week, meetings, paper path), and **act** (open the right URL without hunting). Success: open the host, see what needs you, hit the link, move on. Cloud save via Worker + D1; session cookie after one key paste.

Hybrid intent: **product tool first**, still recognizably a **PHAROS artifact**. Visual system is **Dual Register C** — this surface is **Paper Pressure** (paper + violet beam); public site stays **Cool Beacon**. See `BRAND_IDENTITY.md` and `DESIGN.md`.

## Brand Personality

**Quiet, exact, urgent.**

- Quiet: short labels, no chrome noise, empty states that say little.
- Exact: names that mean the place to act (Where to act, Go, Mail, Paper path).
- Urgent: pressure lives in *Needs you* and action links, not in badges or animation.

Voice stays plain. Diamond Eyes cut over marketing polish.

## Anti-references

- **Notion / second-brain sprawl** — nested pages, empty databases, template theater.
- **SaaS dashboard cliché** — metric hero cards, chart grids, heavy sidebar chrome, purple-gradient marketing UI.
- **Toy to-do apps** — confetti, gamified checkmarks, playful stickers, over-rounded “fun” chrome.
- **Tech-jargon shell** — D1/HttpOnly/Cloudflare in the UI; operator language only.

## Design Principles

1. **Link is the unit of work** — Every actionable item should surface where to act; paste may mint buttons.
2. **Pressure on needs, quiet elsewhere** — One charged zone (*What needs you*); the rest recedes.
3. **PHAROS grain without costume** — Paper surface + purple accent + light serif title; no faux-luxury or brand cosplay.
4. **Capture without ceremony** — Add → name → link → save. No wizards.
5. **Hybrid, not hybridized** — Tool reliability first; PHAROS identity is atmosphere, not a second product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

**Solid defaults (explain simply):** readable text on the paper background, clear keyboard focus, usable without a mouse, and no motion that must run for content to appear. Prefer WCAG **AA** contrast for body and controls. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No formal AAA audit required unless that later becomes a hard gate.
