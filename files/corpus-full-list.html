PHAROS · Martin Lepage, PhD · Montréal

The Corpus, Normalized

Seventy-three works in the four groups the archive now uses — Before Pharos, During, Books, Fictions — each with a précis of what it argues and a statement of the job it does in forming the method.

Where the précis come from. Thirty-six are condensed from the manuscript's own abstract, read directly from the submission copy. The rest have no abstract on file — they are published articles, books, or novels held as .docx and .pdf — and are described from the register's evidence instead. Every one of those is marked. Nothing here is a summary of a document that was not opened.

00Mort et Naissance de Christophe Ulric et L'Ombre et le Double d'Yvon RivardM.A. Laval · 2010
What it argues

Two Rivard novels are structured by an Egyptian triad from the Livre des Morts — âme / double / ombre, ba / ka / khaibit — in which each term holds one fixed actantial function: the âme is destinataire and objet, the double adjuvant, the ombre opposant et adjuvant. No abstract on file; described from the thesis's own chapters and bibliography.

Role in the formation

It is the architecture. A three-part structure in fixed order whose terms cannot be substituted is the thesis's finding in 2010 and the three-agent stack's governing constraint in 2026. Four products cite it, two by page number; 67 vault notes link to it.
