What the order shows

The arc is real, and runs the direction the corpus claims

Stage I studies authority where it has no institutional backing. Stage VI asks when a system's evidence about itself can be trusted. Those are the same question at two scales, and Stages II–IV are how it travelled.

The order inverts the publication record

Stages I–II hold every published item. Stages III–VI hold almost nothing published, and they are where the argument is. The governance spine has one published paper, and it is a Buffy article. The corpus's public identity and its stated centre are not the same thing — anyone meeting this work from outside meets the ethnographer first.

Three works carry the joints

Position 26 turns cultural studies toward machine autonomy. Position 41 supplies the vocabulary everything downstream inherits. Position 63 states the condition under which the method can be believed. A reader given only those three would have the arc.
